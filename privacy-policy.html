Ember Merge — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026
App: Ember Merge (Android, package com.taliworks.embermerge)
Developer: Ember Forge Works
Contact: emberforgeworks@gmail.com

Summary

Ember Merge is an offline puzzle game. We (the developer) do not collect, store, or receive any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The only third-party service in the app is Google AdMob, which shows ads and is described below.

Information stored on your device

Game progress (stages cleared, stars, scores, streaks), selected skin, and settings (sound, music, haptics) are saved locally on your device only. This data is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or using “Reset progress” deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ember Merge shows ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), provided by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version, language)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interaction data (such as ad views and taps) and diagnostic/crash data related to ads

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, under Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and your choices

  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can decline, and you may then see non-personalized ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • The game is fully playable without interacting with ads. Rewarded ads (to refill an undo or shuffle) are always optional. If you own the optional No Ads purchase, no ads of any kind are shown or loaded (and the ads SDK is not started), and refills are free.

Children

Ember Merge is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell or share personal information. Apart from the AdMob processing described above, no data leaves your device.

Security

Data exchanged with Google's ad services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or requests: emberforgeworks@gmail.com

In-app purchases

The optional "No Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details. The app stores only an on-device flag saying the purchase is owned, and it re-checks this with Google Play at launch.
