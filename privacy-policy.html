Ember Merge — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026
App: Ember Merge (Android)
Developer: Ember Forge Works
Contact: emberforgeworks@gmail.com

Summary

Ember Merge is a puzzle game you can play fully as a guest. We (the developer) do not run our own servers and do not receive your personal information. The app uses two Google services: optional Google Play Games Services sign-in (for cloud save and achievements) and Google AdMob (for ads), both described below.

Information stored on your device

Game progress (stages cleared, stars, scores, best combo, saved boards), selected skin, and settings (sound, music, haptics) are saved locally on your device only. This data is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or using “Reset progress” deletes it from your device.

Google Play Games Services (optional sign-in)

Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. You can choose “Continue as guest” and play everything without signing in. If you sign in:

  • What is used: your Play Games player ID and display name (gamer profile), provided by Google.
  • Why: to save your game progress to the cloud (so it can be restored or merged on another device) and to unlock and show achievements.
  • Cloud save: your game progress (scores, stars, unlocked stages and skins, best combo, achievement progress, No Ads ownership flag) is stored by Google Play Games Saved Games in your Google account. It is not stored on any server run by us.
  • This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Games terms.

Deleting your data

  • Stop syncing: use “Sign out” in the app's Settings. The app then stops uploading progress and reporting achievements.
  • Delete cloud save and Play Games data: open the Google Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data (you can delete the data for Ember Merge only, or your whole Play Games profile). You can also manage this from your Google account.
  • Delete on-device data: use “Reset progress” in Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Help: email emberforgeworks@gmail.com and we will help you with any deletion request.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ember Merge shows ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), provided by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version, language)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interaction data (such as ad views and taps) and diagnostic/crash data related to ads

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, under Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and your choices

  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can decline, and you may then see non-personalized ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • The game is fully playable without interacting with ads. Rewarded ads (to refill an undo or shuffle) are always optional. If you own the optional No Ads purchase, no ads of any kind are shown or loaded (and the ads SDK is not started), and refills are free.

Children

Ember Merge is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone other than Google as described above (Google Play Games Services, if you choose to sign in, and Google AdMob for ads). If you play as a guest, only the AdMob processing described above leaves your device.

Security

Data exchanged with Google Play Games Services and Google's ad services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

In-app purchases

The optional "No Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details. The app stores only an on-device flag saying the purchase is owned, and it re-checks this with Google Play at launch.
